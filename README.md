# My Dearest Journey

https://github.com/hahavb/for-gala-cherry-blossom.git



Redesign the existing website into a deeply emotional and beautiful interactive love experience for someone who is extremely special to me



IMPORTANT: Keep the existing background rolling animation exactly as it is and do not remove or break it  Build the new design around that animation and make it feel like part of the whole experience



Remove ALL existing messages and replace them with completely new content focused on how much she means to me  Do NOT make the website centered around Good Morning  Instead make the entire experience about I love you so much  thank you for everything  you are incredibly special to me  and how grateful I am that she is in my life



The website should feel personal emotional warm dreamy and magical rather than looking like a generic romantic template



Create multiple sections that reveal themselves through scrolling and interactions



Section 1 should be a beautiful opening screen with a subtle animated entrance and a message like



"You probably don't even realize how special you are to me"



Then slowly reveal another line



"But somehow you became one of the most important people in my life"



Section 2 should focus on gratitude



Use emotional messages about how thankful I am for her presence  her time  her patience  her care  the memories we have created and all the little things she does that make me happy



Make the text appear with smooth fade up animations as the user scrolls



Section 3 should be about how unique she is to me



Create an interactive floating card or glass panel with a message explaining that she is not just another person in my life  she has a place that nobody else can replace



Use elegant typography and subtle glowing particles around the card



Section 4 should be a large emotional "I LOVE YOU" moment



Make the words appear slowly with cinematic animation instead of simply showing them immediately



Add animated light particles  soft glow  depth  blur and subtle scaling



Under it add something like



"I don't think I'll ever have enough words to explain how much you mean to me"



Section 5 should be a gratitude timeline



Instead of dates use emotional moments such as



"Thank you for staying"

"Thank you for understanding me"

"Thank you for making me smile"

"Thank you for being patient with me"

"Thank you for simply being you"



Each card should have its own animation and texture



Section 6 should be an interactive memory style section



Create floating cards that move slightly with the cursor or touch  Each card can reveal a short emotional sentence when tapped



Make the interactions feel smooth and premium



Section 7 should be a final emotional message



Create a large cinematic section where everything slowly becomes calmer and the background animation remains visible



Text:



"I just want you to know that I love you so much"



Then reveal:



"Thank you for everything you've done for me"

"Thank you for being there"

"Thank you for becoming such a special part of my life"



Finally reveal:



"You're genuinely one of the most precious people in my life"



Then add a small final button called



"One more thing"



When clicked it should reveal



"I love you more than I can put into words"
